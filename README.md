## 📌 Overview
This repository contains the deliverable for **Task 2** of the CodeAlpha Cybersecurity Internship — a Phishing Awareness Training module designed to educate employees (HR and Marketing teams) on identifying and avoiding phishing attacks.

## Objective
- Create a presentation/training module focused on phishing attacks
- Explain how to recognize phishing emails and fake websites
- Educate on common social engineering tactics used by attackers
- Provide practical best practices and prevention tips
- Include real-world examples and up-to-date statistics for better engagement

## Deliverable
- **File:** `Phishing_Awareness_Training.pptx`
- **Audience:** HR and Marketing teams
- **Content highlights:** Real 2026 phishing statistics, red-flag identification checklist, social engineering tactics, and prevention best practices

## Tools & Skills Used
- Presentation design (Microsoft PowerPoint)
- Security awareness content research
- Cybersecurity fundamentals (phishing, social engineering)
